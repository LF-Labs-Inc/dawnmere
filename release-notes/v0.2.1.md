# Dawnmere 0.2.1

Search that reads Chinese and Japanese. Update with `dawnmere update`, or run the install line again.

- **Search in Chinese and Japanese.** When an app searches your memory, questions and notes in Chinese or Japanese are now read by two models made for many languages, on your Mac. A note asked about in different words is found about as often as in English: 40 of 64 reworded questions in our test (Japanese 21 of 32, Chinese 19 of 32), against 6 of 64 in 0.2.0 and 39 of 59 for English. Asked in the note's own words: 64 of 64 (0.2.0: 38).
- **Not changed: what reaches your prompt unasked.** In Codex and Claude Code a note still comes up on its own when you ask with its words (63 of 64), and mostly does not when you word the question differently (2 of 64; 0.2.0: 3 of 64). Ask the app to search and it finds it. Fewer wrong notes come up this way (0 in our test, 6 before), and everyday prompts that need no memory get none.
- **Not changed: across languages.** A Japanese or Chinese question about a note you saved in English is still mostly missed unless a model is set up (then it is also searched in English, as before), and an English question about a Japanese or Chinese note is found as often as in 0.2.0.
- **A download only if you use it.** The two models (about 271 MB) are not part of setup. The first time you save or ask something in Chinese or Japanese, Dawnmere downloads them in the background from this repo's models release and checks every file; until then Chinese and Japanese work as in 0.2.0. `dawnmere doctor` says whether they are there, `dawnmere models` lists every model, and `dawnmere models --get embed-ml rerank-ml` downloads them now.
- **English is unchanged.** English questions never use the new models, and an English-only Mac never downloads them. On 2,080 English prompts, what Dawnmere adds to a prompt and what search returns are byte for byte what 0.2.0 gives.

Engine 0.1.15. The connector is unchanged (0.1.2).
